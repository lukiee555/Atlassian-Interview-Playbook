Round 1 · AI code design

P40 drill priority

After one current-format task, rehearse Agent Rating and Middleware Router first because they recur in direct P40 reports. Add API Rate Limiter for the newest Q3 2026 P40 code-quality signal, then Snake Game for stateful object design.

Round 3 · Management reset

0–2

Select the story

Complex → pricing fallback. Initiative → Spark. Feedback → visibility. Failure → international deployment. Conflict → incident/business rule. Mentor → one-sprint coaching. Team → triage. Customer → pricing/Promo UI.

2–5

Give the core

Business need, personal scope, difficult decision, three actions, verified result and learning.

5–8

Defend it

What was yours? Why was the other option reasonable? Who disagreed? What failed? What changed permanently?

8–10

Close

Hands-on technical-lead direction and why the full-stack role is the natural next step.

Round 4 · Values reset

0–2

Map the value

Open → disagreement. Heart → production miss/recovery (backup: pricing trade-off). Customer → retry/UI/streaming. Team → mentoring/triage. Change → Spark.

2–4

Answer in 90 seconds

Real tension, your responsibility, three observable actions, result and learning.

4–8

Probe behavior

What exactly did you say? Why was the other view reasonable? What did you give up? Who became more effective?

8–10

Truth audit

Remove unverified numbers, invented conflict and excessive technical detail.
