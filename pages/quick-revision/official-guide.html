Official interview guide: what to optimize for

Use this page when you want the official signal without candidate-report noise. Your recruiter-specific four-round path still takes precedence over the generic staged process in the guide.

On this page

AI-enabled Code Design

60 minutes + screen share

Existing repository in HackerRank with AI coding assistants; feature work, bug fixing or refactoring. Deep language expertise is not the target.

Communication

Your thinking is as important as the final result. Narrate assumptions, evidence and why you accept or reject AI suggestions.

TDD is explicit

Testing is described as crucial, with TDD specifically recommended. Baseline → failing behavior test → minimum fix → refactor → regression.

Debugger + alternatives

Debugger use is encouraged. Show alternative solutions and explain why your selected approach fits best.

System Design

60-minute progression

Seek first to understand. Clarify upfront, start from fundamentals, build complexity progressively, communicate continuously, and cover performance, reliability, scale and customer impact. Draw from your own production experience when it informs a decision.
