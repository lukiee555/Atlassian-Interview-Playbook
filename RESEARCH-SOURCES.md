## Personalized experience basis

The management and values preparation uses user-confirmed experience from the Resume Grill project and the supplied pricing design document:

- End-to-end implementation and deployment of a safer multi-stage pricing fallback and derived national-price aggregation flow.
- Explicit normal, replay-only and emergency operating modes; stage-aware routing; current-state lookup; no-ratchet derivation; per-offer isolation; retry and idempotency analysis.
- Consolidation of five Spark jobs into one pipeline handling more than 165 million records.
- Staged API/control, parallel-comparison and database/target migration modes.
- International pricing integrations and tenant-context propagation.
- International production deployment failure after a Java upgrade due to a market-specific runtime-option parity gap.
- Production disagreement in which temporary containment was accepted while the permanent business behavior was confirmed with business owners.
- Cross-team defect triage during a large rollout.
- Mentoring junior, vendor and frontend engineers, including one-sprint coaching around communication and implementation ownership.
- Constructive feedback that backend judgment was not sufficiently visible, followed by design documents, presentations and senior-engineer reviews.
- Promo-pricing operations UI implemented, tested and deployed within two days rather than handing non-engineering users a script.
- Wally mobile/full-stack ownership and the decision to use streaming responses for a more responsive AI experience.

The public website deliberately generalizes proprietary identifiers. Exact metrics and interpersonal outcomes that are not confirmed remain qualitative or visibly marked for verification.
